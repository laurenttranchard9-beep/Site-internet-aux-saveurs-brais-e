Frequently wagged questions

How long does a groom take?

Most sessions take 1.5–3 hours depending on size and coat. We'll text you when your pet is ready.

My pet is nervous. Can you help?

Absolutely. Our groomers are Fear-Free certified. We go slowly, take breaks and use lots of praise and treats.

Do you need vaccination records?

Yes — please bring proof of up-to-date rabies vaccination on your first visit (and bordetella for dogs).

What products do you use?

Gentle, pH-balanced, tear-free shampoos. Hypoallergenic options are free on request.
